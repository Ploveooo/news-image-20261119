## 1. World Court — ICJ rules on Myanmar genocide case

- The International Court of Justice delivered its judgment on 19 November
  in the case brought by Gambia in 2019, accusing Myanmar of genocide
  against the Rohingya Muslim minority after the 2017 offensive that drove
  more than 700,000 Rohingya into Bangladesh.
- The judgment was read by the court's president, Judge Yuji Iwasawa, at a
  public sitting at the Peace Palace in The Hague; it is final, with no
  right of appeal. If the court finds genocide, Myanmar would be the first
  state held responsible under the 1948 Genocide Convention — the ICJ has
  never before found a state responsible for genocide.
- Sources: Reuters via news.az (3 Nov 2026 announcement; ruling 19 Nov);
  Human Rights Watch via Voice Gambia; Al Jazeera / Courrier
  International via Pollar News (18 Nov 2026).

## 3. Games — GTA VI launches to a 99 on Metacritic

## 4. Africa — Ethiopia and Eritrea mass troops at Badme

- Ethiopia and Eritrea massed forces along their shared border, bringing
  the two armies within striking distance of each other near the disputed
  town of Badme, fuelling fears of outright war between the historical
  rivals.
- Conflict monitors ACLED and the International Crisis Group reported

  fire; Reuters first reported the build-up on 18 November.
- Sources: Reuters via The Hindu (18 Nov 2026); ACLED statement
  (17 Nov 2026); ReliefWeb Atrocity Alert No. 503 (19 Nov 2026).

## 5. Gaza — Israeli tanks cross Gaza's Yellow Line

- Israeli forces launched a rare ground operation in central Gaza on
  18 November, advancing west of the ceasefire's "Yellow Line" near the
  Nuseirat and Bureij refugee camps — the first ground crossing of the
  line since the October 2025 ceasefire.
- The army said the operation aimed to dismantle a tunnel that posed a
  threat to troops; dozens of displaced Palestinians fled, and Gaza's
  civil defence said around 10,000 people had been displaced since the
  operation began.
- Sources: Reuters via GMA News (19 Nov 2026); AFP via Malay Mail
  (19 Nov 2026); NDTV (19 Nov 2026); China Daily (19 Nov 2026).

## 6. Security — IAEA: North Korea expanded Yongbyon plant

- IAEA Director General Rafael Grossi told the agency's Board of
  Governors on 18 November that North Korea had completed the exterior of
  another extension to its original uranium-enrichment facility at
  Yongbyon, built from mid-September to late October 2026, expanding
  production of weapons-grade material.
- The IAEA has had no access to North Korea since 2009 and monitors the
  programme by satellite imagery; Grossi said the ongoing expansion
  "remains a cause for serious concern".
- Sources: Reuters (18 Nov 2026); The Straits Times (18 Nov 2026);
  Arab News (18 Nov 2026); Korea Times (18 Nov 2026).
